# Where to list besides Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo (9 October 2026)

Research from web searches, mostly vendor blogs. Fees change; confirm each on the platform's own
host page before pricing. Not yet checked against any account.

## Channels Uplisting connects to (per Uplisting's own pages)
Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo (Expedia Group), **Google Vacation Rentals**, and a **free direct-booking website** (Stripe payments).
- Vrbo through Uplisting: payments go through Uplisting/Stripe, Instant Book on, and European hosts need a registered company or tax ID.
- Google through Uplisting: needs Stripe and Instant Book.
- No direct Expedia, Holidu, Plum Guide or mid-term channels were found in Uplisting's list. Other channel managers may add them.

## Worth adding for traffic
| Channel | Why | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Vacation Rentals | Huge search traffic; guests land on a booking site | No listing fee or commission from Google; you pay your channel manager | Metasearch only. If the guest books through an OTA that OTA's commission applies; a direct site avoids it. Price and availability must match |
| Direct booking site (Uplisting free site) | No commission; repeat guests; fed by Google | Stripe card fees | Needs Stripe set up |
| Expedia | Large OTA, separate audience from Booking.com | About 15-20% (one source) | Vrbo is Expedia Group, so some overlap |
| Plum Guide | Curated, higher-end guests; London based | Not confirmed | Selective, only suits quality properties; apply, not open listing |
| Holidu | Metasearch across many holiday sites | Not confirmed | Vendor blog, check terms |
| TripAdvisor rentals | Large audience | One source: 3% host + guest fee | Check it still takes bookings |

## Mid-term and corporate (30+ nights): a different market
Useful in Edinburgh because the council treats furnished lets of 30 days or more outside the short-let rules (**confirm with the council**).
- **Spotahome, Housing Anywhere, Homelike, Flatio:** mid/long stays; the guest pays a booking fee; older figures about EUR 150-250.
- **Contractor and corporate networks:** Comfy Workers, StayForWork, The Home Makers Hub, SilverDoor, Blueground-style corporate platforms, Corporate Lets Scotland. Usually invoiced, account managers, bookings of weeks to months. Often the most reliable income for flats that sit empty midweek.
- **Furnished Finder** is US healthcare-focused; not relevant for the UK.

## Scotland and Edinburgh rules that affect every listing
- A **short-term let licence is required before taking bookings**, and the **licence number must appear on every advert**. Operating without one is an offence (fines up to £2,500).
- All of Edinburgh is a **Short-Term Let Control Area**: planning permission is generally needed for secondary lets (not your main home), on top of the licence.
- **Edinburgh visitor levy from 24 July 2026:** 5% on the first five paid nights of a stay, collected by the host and paid to the council (confirm scope).
- Make sure each property has its licence and planning status recorded before listing anywhere. Worth adding a "licence number" field and expiry reminder to property records.

## Suggested order
1. Airbnb + Booking.com + Vrbo (through the channel manager) so bookings, payouts and cleans flow.
2. Google Vacation Rentals and the free direct site (cheapest extra traffic, via Uplisting).
3. Expedia if the channel manager supports it.
4. Corporate/contractor networks and a mid-term channel for midweek and quiet-season gaps.
5. Plum Guide only for the best properties.
