# Where to list besides Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo (9 October 2026)

Research from web searches, mostly vendor blogs. Fees change; confirm each on the platform's own
host page before pricing. Not yet checked against any account.

## Channels Uplisting connects to (per Uplisting's own pages)
Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo (Expedia Group), **Google Vacation Rentals**, and a **free direct-booking website** (Stripe payments).
- Vrbo through Uplisting: payments go through Uplisting/Stripe, Instant Book on, and European hosts need a registered company or tax ID.
- Google through Uplisting: needs Stripe and Instant Book.
- No direct Expedia, Holidu, Plum Guide or mid-term channels were found in Uplisting's list. Other channel managers may add them.

keeps from a £100 stay (accommodation charge incl. cleaning fee) after the platform's cut, before
the channel manager and tax. Figures are estimates from public sources; the platform's signed
terms are what count.

## Channel manager cost (per month, estimates)
Uplisting: about £72 for up to 5 properties, or about £16 per property for 6-40 (tracker read of its
pricing page on 3 Sep 2026). Another source says £99 for up to 15. Examples: 5 properties about
£72; 10 about £160; 20 about £320. This is on top of every commission above. **Confirm the live
price.** Some channels may add Stripe charges for the direct site and Google.

## Mid-term and corporate (30+ nights): a different market
Useful in Edinburgh because the council treats furnished lets of 30 days or more outside the short-let rules (**confirm with the council**).
- **Spotahome, Housing Anywhere, Homelike, Flatio:** mid/long stays. The guest pays the booking fee (older figures about EUR 150-250, Housing Anywhere about 25% of rent capped in that range); the host cost is not confirmed, so ask before listing.
- **Contractor and corporate networks:** Comfy Workers charges the host **15% + VAT** (from 1 Jan 2024), so about **18% all in** unless the VAT is recoverable: keep about £82 per £100 invoiced. StayForWork, The Home Makers Hub, SilverDoor, Blueground-style platforms and Corporate Lets Scotland: host terms not found, ask for them. Usually invoiced, with account managers and stays of weeks to months. Often the most reliable income for flats that sit empty midweek.
- **Furnished Finder** is US healthcare-focused; not relevant for the UK.

## Scotland and Edinburgh rules that affect every listing
- A **short-term let licence is required before taking bookings**, and the **licence number must appear on every advert**. Operating without one is an offence (fines up to £2,500).
- All of Edinburgh is a **Short-Term Let Control Area**: planning permission is generally needed for secondary lets (not your main home), on top of the licence.
- **Edinburgh visitor levy from 24 July 2026:** 5% on the first five paid nights of a stay, collected by the host and paid to the council (confirm scope).
- Make sure each property has its licence and planning status recorded before listing anywhere. Worth adding a "licence number" field and expiry reminder to property records.

## Suggested order (with what each step costs)
1. Airbnb + Booking.com + Vrbo through the channel manager: about 8-22% commission, plus about £16 per property per month for Uplisting.
2. Google Vacation Rentals and the free direct site: no Google fee, Stripe about 1.5-3.25% + 20p per booking; the cheapest extra traffic.
3. Expedia if the channel manager supports it: about 15-20%.
4. Corporate/contractor networks (Comfy Workers about 18% all in) and a mid-term channel for midweek and quiet gaps.
5. Plum Guide only for the best properties: 3-16.5% plus about £300 onboarding each.
