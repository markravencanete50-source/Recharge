Set-up

Before you start

  1. The super admin makes one test account for each role: in the app, go to Work → Management → Access control → Add a person, enter the tester's name and email, choose the role (Owner, Admin, Stays, Cleaner, Cleaning manager, Ready or Accounts) and press "Add and make link".
  2. The super admin copies the one-time set-password link and sends it to the tester privately. Each link works once and for a limited time; if it runs out, open the person in Access control and choose "Set-password link" for a new one.
  3. The tester opens the link, presses "Choose my password", chooses a password of 12 or more characters (using the eye button to check what they typed) and lands straight in their workspace.
  4. To sign in later, open the app's /workspace address (the button above) and sign in with your email and password.
  5. Use a clearly fake test property and guest, for example "Test – Leith flat", so test work is easy to spot and tidy away afterwards.
  6. To be signed in as two roles at once, use a different browser for each. Keep this checklist open in the same browser each time, because ticks are saved in that browser only.

Please never send passwords or keys over WhatsApp or email. Each tester chooses their own password from their link. If someone is locked out, the super admin makes them a new set-password link instead.

Suggested order: Sign-in and invites, Owner, Super admin / Admin, Stays, Cleaning manager, Cleaner, Ready, Accounts. Some steps need another role to act first; each section says which.
