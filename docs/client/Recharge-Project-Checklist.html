New this round

Built from the feature audit (9 October 2026)

We went through the app role by role and filled the gaps we found. Everything here is live today and needs no outside service. To try it yourself, use the role testing checklist that comes with this page.

Signing in

New sign-in screenNew

Working

Sign-in, password reset, choosing a password and invites now share one design: a forest-green panel with gentle motion (activity cards, a floating house and team chips) beside a bone-coloured card. It stays forest green in dark mode.

See what you typedNew

Working

Every password box has an eye button to show or hide the password. Boxes no longer turn grey when the browser fills them in.

Invites sign you straight inNew

Working

Choosing a password from an invite or reset link signs the person in and opens their workspace. The page shows which email the account is for, and checks the password as it is typed: 12 or more characters, and both boxes match.

Owners

A bottom bar made for ownersNew

Working

Home, Properties, Inbox (the raised button in the middle, which opens Messages), Approvals and Reports. On a computer, the side bar adds Tools.

ApprovalsNew

Working

Every repair waiting for the owner, with the property, what is wrong, the quote, any earlier quote and why it changed, and the owner's limit. They Approve, Decline (with a reason) or Message Recharge. Onboarding steps waiting for them (the estimate, the agreement, the set-up form) are listed too. When nothing is waiting it says "Nothing is waiting for you".

When an owner declines a repairNew

Working

The repair shows "Owner declined" with their reason, and Stays and admins are told. Stays or an admin can get a new quote, which goes back to the owner, or close the repair.

Owner HomeNew

Working

Tiles for what's waiting for them, new messages and this month's earnings; each property's onboarding progress with its next step; and Start a new enquiry.

Reports with a stays calendarNew

Working

Earnings, occupancy and average nightly rate for any month. A month calendar shows each property's stays as coloured bars: tap a stay for its details, or a day for its arrivals and departures. Below are guests staying now, upcoming bookings, earnings by property, a 12-month earnings chart and statements.

Reminders that keep things movingNew

Working

If a repair or estimate has waited on the owner for 48 hours, they are reminded in the app. At 96 hours client relations (or admins) are told.

Admins and management

Admin Home: across the businessNew

Working

Cards for Stays, Cleans, Ready and Accounts. Each line opens the right place in Work.

Needs a management decisionNew

Working

One list on Admin Home: contested cleans (Uphold or Overturn), second quality warnings, quotes over the limit (Approve or Decline), unusual owner payouts (Approve or Hold), owner approvals that have stalled ("Remind the owner now", once a day) and sign-offs missed yesterday ("Mark as seen").

Management spend limitNew

Working

An optional limit for repairs, off to start, set in Accounts or Management. Above it, a repair also needs an admin's approval after the owner's.

Unusual payouts flaggedNew

Working

An owner payout is flagged when it is negative, or more than half above or below that property's average over its last three statements.

Missed sign-offsNew

Working

Each morning after 8am, any Stays sign-off or listing check missed the day before becomes a "Missed yesterday" item, and admins are told.

History and the Decisions reportNew

Working

Every job has a History: status changes, steps, reviews, overturns, expense decisions and escalations (owners don't see internal escalations). Management → Decisions lists the last 90 days of decisions, filtered by area, with a spreadsheet download.

Training on HomeNew

Working

Every team role has a Training tile on Home. For admins it opens the course uploads.

Calendar and messages

A calendar for every team roleNew

Working

Work → Calendar for admins, Stays, cleaners, cleaning managers, Ready and Accounts. A month view with Today and arrows; each day lists departures, cleans, arrivals, repairs, routine visits, inspections and other dated work, plus payment runs for admins and Accounts. Admins and cleaning managers can pick a person. Tap an entry to open it, message the property team or open statements.

One inbox with tabsNew

Working

Messages has Guests, Clients and Team tabs, each with a count, and opens on the tab that needs attention. The badge at the top counts guests waiting.

Saved repliesNew

Working

Ready-made replies for guests and owners, with the guest's name and the property filled in. Admins and Stays add, edit and remove them.

Message the property teamNew

Working

A button on cleans, repairs, routine visits and calendar entries opens one group chat for the property or job, with the Stays and Ready staff, the cleaning managers and the person doing the job.

Cleaners and cleaning managers

A fair rejection ruleNew

Working

A cleaner goes to management review only after two upheld rejections in the same month. A rejection overturned on appeal doesn't count.

Cleaners kept informedNew

Working

Cleaners are told when a clean is accepted or rejected and when a dispute is decided. The clean shows the reason, the decision and management's note, and "Give your side" while a dispute is open. Work → Cleans lists disputes decided this month.

Disputes decided by cleaning managersNew

Working

Cleaning managers decide disputes for the properties they manage (admins for any). They see the report, checklist, photos, the reason, guest feedback and the cleaner's statement. There is a Disputes tool on the cleaning manager's Home.

Pay rate and "Your month"New

Working

Admins or the cleaner's cleaning manager set a pay rate per clean. The cleaner's Home shows their month: cleans accepted and rejected, any warning, and the amount so far (or "Rate not set yet"). Accounts see the amount on each cleaner's invoice, with the total filled in.

Who's leaving and who's nextNew

Working

Each clean shows how many guests are leaving, and the next arrival date and guest count (no names).

Report a problemNew

Working

Inside an open clean, a cleaner can report a problem with photos. It becomes a repair for the Stays team, and the clean keeps its ticks, report and photos.

Accounts and Ready

Photo evidence on expensesNew

Working

Supplier invoices and before and after photos can be added to expenses and delivered purchases (pictures only; screenshot a PDF). An expense can be linked to a finished repair or delivered purchase with "Use the finished job as proof".

Draft expenses from finished repairsNew

Working

A completed repair makes a draft expense with the contractor, the actual cost, the report and the before and after photos. Accounts add the invoice number and send it for approval.

Record the supplier invoiceNew

Working

On a delivered purchase, Accounts record the supplier invoice (amount, reference, photo, paid) and then reconcile and close it.

Statements to download or printNew

Working

Owner statements as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a branded printable page, for Accounts and for the owner. The payout upload step has example Airbnb and Booking.com files.

Ready HomeNew

Working

Repairs by stage, from owner approval through to paid; tap a stage to open those repairs. This month's routine visits per property show as done, due or overdue.

Routine visits made automaticallyNew

Working

Visits for this month and next are created by themselves, with a reminder three days before each is due. Ready can read the access instructions (not logins or finance) for their properties from "Access and logins".

Stays

Today's routineNew

Working

A card at the top of Stays: listing checks done, empty nights to price, repairs to refer, today's cleans, open AirCover claims, open direct-booking offers, and the weekly reports on Mondays.

Price these nightsNew

Working

An empty-nights ticket opens the pricing in Stays with one tap.

Direct-booking invoiceNew
