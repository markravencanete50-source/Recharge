The app on your phone

Easier to find, faster to open

This round focused on the app as people use it every day on their phones: getting to their tools in one tap, opening quickly, and feeling responsive.

Your tools on Home, for every roleNew

Working

Home now opens with a "Your tools" grid made for each person's role. One tap goes straight to the right screen, tab or section, so nobody has to search through menus. "All tools" lists everything else on their account.

Shortcuts that open the form straight awayNew

Working

Under the tools, each role has buttons for what they start most: Record a stay, Report a repair, New clean, New expense or New routine visit. One tap opens the form ready to fill in.

Always up to dateNew

Working

The app checks for changes every minute while it is open, and as soon as someone comes back to it. A new onboarding stage, task or update appears without anyone tapping refresh. It waits while someone is filling in a form.

Illustrated icons for every toolNew

Working

Each tool has its own colour illustration in the Recharge palette: a price tag for pricing, a signed sheet for sign-off, a shield for AirCover, a parcel for restock requests and so on. The same icons appear in the full tools list.

Opens much fasterNew

Working

What a phone downloads to open the app has dropped from about 6 MB to 2.7 MB: smaller logo and font files, and the old sample-data preview removed from the live app. After the first visit, the phone keeps these files, so later visits open almost straight away. The battery intro now plays once a day and has a Skip button, and the app loads underneath it.

More motion and feedbackNew

Working

Every save now shows a short animated confirmation, such as "Saved", "Day signed off" or "Clocked in". Tool tiles lift when tapped, checklists tick with a small spring, the section a tool opens briefly glows, and screens slide in as you move between them. People who prefer less motion can turn it off in Profile and preferences.

Fixes for small screensNew

Working

Rows of buttons that ran off the edge of the screen now wrap onto a second line. Choices such as departments and tabs wrap so every option is visible without scrolling sideways. The round Help button no longer sits over Home or on screens whose bottom bar already has Help, and it slides out of the way while you scroll. The browser tab now says "Recharge".

We checked this on phone, narrow-phone and desktop screens, in light and dark mode, for every role. Unused code was removed, and all 330 automated checks pass.

Owners

Property onboarding and the owner app

Owners sign in to see their properties, every stage of their onboarding, upcoming stays, statements and repairs. The full onboarding journey runs inside the app:

Enquiry→Estimate with comparables→Owner decides→Management agreement signed→Set-up form→Onboarding invoice→Inspection→Live

Estimates and the owner's decision

Working

The team publishes an estimate with low, expected and high nightly rates and links to comparable listings. The owner approves, asks for more time (the team is reminded on the follow-up date), or declines (the property is archived on both sides).

Explore your estimateNew

Working

Owners pick a quieter, expected or strong year and an occupancy level, and their earnings update as they go. A "Booked direct" switch shows how much they save in platform fees on direct bookings. The team sets the occupancy, direct-booking share, platform fee and management fee when publishing the estimate.

What Recharge does, extra services and an FAQNew

Working

Next to the estimate, owners read what Recharge does, the extra services, how onboarding works, and answers to the questions owners usually ask (the estimate, more time, repairs, payouts and post). The wording sits in one place, so we can change it to your exact words.

Ask Recharge a questionNew

Working

At any stage the owner can ask a question on their onboarding page. The team is told and answers on the same page.

Agreement and set-up form

Working

The owner signs the management agreement in the app; their name, the date and the exact wording are kept. The set-up form captures bank details, access, utilities, mail preference, warranty and repair contacts, the repair amount Recharge can approve without asking, furnishing and licensing. Those answers create the follow-up tasks automatically, and the Ready team's answers show on the property page under "Getting ready to go live".

A copy of the signed agreementNew

Working

Once the agreement is signed, the owner and the team can download a copy of the exact wording, with who signed it, when, and which revision. It opens in any browser to keep, print or save as a PDF.

Garden care set up automaticallyNew

Working

Onboarding invoiceNew

Working

Inspection checklistNew

Staff onboarding, shifts and performance

Contract and training before access

Working

New team members sign the staff contract for their role in the app and complete the training marked "required" before any tool opens.

Access control for super adminsNew

Working

One screen lists every account, with when each person last signed in and whether they are signed in now. A super admin can promote someone or move them to another role (for example from Cleaning manager to Admin). They then see exactly what everyone in that role sees, with the same Home, Work and tools, and their app switches over within a minute. Their properties move with them. A super admin can also switch individual tools on or off, sign them out of every device, revoke their password (they set a new one with "Forgot password"), block or unblock them, and delete the account. A deleted account with work history is closed rather than erased, so the record of who did what stays. Every change is logged with who made it. It is in Work → Management → Access control, and on a super admin's Home.

Departments and shiftsNew

Working

Admins give each person a department (guest relations, client relations, cleaning, Ready, Accounts or management) and their usual shift: early (8am to 4pm), late (3pm to 11pm) or both. Clock-in starts from their department, and both show on their shift card and in Team performance.

Clock in and clock out

Working

Every team member clocks in and out from Home. Admins see who is on shift and can end a shift someone forgot to close.

Team performance reportNew

Working

For today, the last 7 or 30 days: each person's replies, hours, listing checks, reviews logged, pricing decisions and daily sign-offs, whether they are on track, and their bonus tier. Admins also see the last 14 days of Stays sign-offs.

Guest reply times and bonus tiers

Built, waiting on the channel manager

Every guest message is timed: green within 5 minutes, amber within 10, red after that, credited to whoever was clocked in to guest relations. 90% green is on track; 95% and 100% unlock the bonus tiers. The figures fill in as soon as guest messages flow in.

Client relations score

Needs the shared mailboxes

Client relations can already be set as a department. Their reply score needs owner emails, which come with the shared mailboxes.

Stays

Listings, pricing, licensing and repairs

Log a reviewNew

Working

On each property, Stays log a guest review: platform, rating, good, mixed or bad, what the guest said and how it was handled. A really bad review can be marked "re-list". Until the channel manager feeds reviews in, they are entered by hand.

Re-listingsNew

Working

A review marked "re-list", or a listing check that finds a listing hidden or suspended, goes on the re-listing list on the Stays screen: to start, under way, then re-listed.

Today's sign-offNew

Working

At the end of each day Stays sign off that all listings are visible, bad reviews are answered, suspensions are dealt with and re-listings are done or under way, with a note for anything still open. Sign-offs count in Team performance.

Empty nights become tickets automaticallyNew

Working

Every live property with an empty night in the coming week gets one "Price the empty nights" ticket. It updates itself as nights are booked or priced and closes once every night is covered. Staff hold or change each price with a reason, and those decisions feed the weekly owner report.

Licensing in stagesNew

Working

Licensing tickets from onboarding run through the full process: application submitted (council and reference), safety checks (gas, electrical, alarms, legionella, EPC and insurance), then licence granted.

Direct bookings

Working

Stays of 7 nights or more get a direct-booking ticket. The offer shows next to the original price, with the 10% off, up to £100 policy. A yes goes to Accounts for the invoice, and "The guest said no" now closes the offer. Bookings from the channel manager will create these tickets once it is connected.

Repairs with types and guided checksNew

Working

Stays choose the repair type (heating, hot water, leak, toilet, electrics, appliance, Wi-Fi, locks, pests, damage or other), link the guest's stay and mark it urgent. Each type has a checklist of safe checks to go through with the guest; the ticked checks travel with the referral to Ready. The owner's preferred repair contact and warranties show on every repair as "contact first".

When a repair quote changesNew

Working

If the real quote differs from the estimate, Ready or Stays update it with the reason. A higher quote above the owner's approval limit goes back to the owner first, and the repair carries on from where it was once they approve. A lower one needs nothing more.

Everyone kept in the loop on repairsNew

Working

Ready are told when a repair is referred. The Stays person who referred it is told when it is booked and when it is done, so they can update the guest. Work over the owner's limit still goes to the owner for approval first.

Logins for each platformNew

Decide

Getting started this week

  1. Open the web app link above on your computer or phone and sign in with your admin account.
  2. Add the staff contract and mark the required courses under Work → Management → Onboarding & training.
  3. Ask each team member to sign up in the app, approve them in People, give them their tools and properties, then set their department and usual shift in Team performance.
  4. Ask Accounts to fill in the budget chart on the Accounts screen, so purchase requests can be checked against it.
  5. Show each team member the "Your tools" grid at the top of Home. It is set up for their role, and "All tools" lists everything else on their account.
  6. Start the first owner enquiry from Properties, or ask the owner to sign up and start it themselves.
  7. Send us the accounts from the list above as they are ready, starting with the channel manager and photo storage.

Every tool on someone's account is listed under Help → Your tools, with a button that opens it, so each person can see exactly what they have and where to find it.
