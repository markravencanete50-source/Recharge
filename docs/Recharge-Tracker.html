What we built and how it works

Grouped by who uses it. Each card says what the person can do, then how it works behind the scenes.

Owners Live

  • Home: what is waiting for you, new messages, this month's earnings, and each property's onboarding progress with the next step. "Start a new enquiry" is here.
  • Properties: each property's details, listings, stays and documents.
  • Inbox: built-in chat with Recharge. Filter chips show what needs a reply.
  • Approvals: one list for repairs over your limit and onboarding steps. Approve, decline with a reason, or message Recharge.
  • Reports: earnings, occupancy and average nightly rate with a period picker; a month calendar with a colour per property; statements; the weekly report; the mailbox with photos of post.
How it works: an owner only ever sees their own properties. A repair above the owner's limit waits on Approvals. After 48 hours the owner gets a reminder, and after 96 hours client relations is told.
Enquiry→Estimate→Decision→Sign agreement→Set-up form→Invoice→Inspection→Live

Stays team Live

  • "Today's routine" card: listing checks, empty nights, repairs to refer, cleans, claims, offers
  • Listing health: log reviews as good, mixed or bad, tag "re-list", and track the re-listing
  • Empty nights in the next 7 days get a pricing ticket automatically; staff hold or change the price with a reason
  • Direct-booking offers (10% off, up to £100) tracked from yes to invoice to confirmed
  • Repairs: choose the type, run the guided checks with the guest, refer to Ready, chase until done
  • Licensing in stages, local events, platform login records (no passwords stored)
  • Guest inbox with saved replies and green, amber and red reply timers
How it works: daily sign-off and daily work count towards each person's score and bonus tier. A sign-off missed yesterday shows on Admin Home after 8am.

Cleaners and cleaning managers Live

  • Today board: Today, Tomorrow and Overdue, with a "Next up" card
  • Checklist: items can require a photo. A clean cannot be submitted until every required photo is attached
  • Offline photos: photos are saved on the device and upload when the signal returns, with a visible status
  • "Report a problem" inside a clean makes a repair for Stays, with photos
  • "Your month": cleans accepted and rejected, and the amount for the invoice
  • Disputes: "Give your side"; the cleaning manager decides for their properties
  • Managers edit checklist templates per property and set pay rates per clean
How it works: the database refuses a clean that is missing its required photos, so this cannot be bypassed by the app. Accepted cleans go on the cleaner's invoice; rejected cleans do not.

Ready team Live

  • Repairs by stage from referral to paid; book the repair and Stays are told
  • Record delivery with photos of what arrived
  • New-property inspection with checklist, issues and photos
  • Routine visits (garden and bins, maintenance) created automatically, with a reminder three days before
  • Read access instructions for their properties, but not logins or finance
How it works: Ready only sees repairs once Stays has referred them. Warranty repairs need proof the provider was contacted first.

Accounts Live

  • Supplier invoices with evidence attached; approval needs proof of the work
  • Purchase requests checked against the budget chart, then the order recorded; Ready proves delivery, then Accounts close it
  • Upload Airbnb and Booking.com payout files and reconcile by reservation code
  • Owner statements: revenue, cleaning, approved costs, 15% management fee and owner payout; spreadsheet and printable page
  • Mark as paid by bank transfer; costs are queued for Xero
How it works: unusual payouts and costs over the limit go to management for a decision before they are paid.

Admins and management Live

  • Admin Home: Stays, Cleans, Ready and Accounts at a glance; "Needs a management decision"
  • Every job has a History; Decisions list for 90 days with a spreadsheet
  • Team performance: shifts, reply times, scores and bonus tiers at 95% and 100%
  • People and invites, super-admin Access control, role changes, training uploads
  • Staff onboarding: contract and role training before access
How it works: only a super admin can change privileged accounts, and the last super admin cannot be removed. Every change is logged.

Everyone Live

  • Sign-in with eye toggles, invite links that sign you straight in, and password reset
  • Calendar by team, built-in group chat for a property or job, Help and FAQ
  • Notifications in the app and on phones and computers through the browser
  • Light and dark mode; installable as a web app
  • Profile: privacy policy, terms, download my data, delete my account
How it works: deleting your account removes your name, email, phone and password at once and shows you as "Deleted user". Business records are kept for the legal period, then removed.

Behind the scenes Live

  • Supabase (Mumbai): database, sign-in and private file storage
  • Cloudflare: the website, the app's server and scheduled jobs
  • Expo app: one codebase for the web app and the phone app
  • Daily job removes expired records (notifications, reset links, old security logs, deleted-account leftovers)
  • Hostaway sync is switched off and old data archived; the channel manager comes later

Security: what is in place, and what must be true before launch

The core design holds up under review. The items below are what we checked, then what still needs to be done by someone with the right account.

In place Sign-in

  • Passwords of 12 or more characters
  • 5 wrong passwords lock the account for 24 hours; recovery lifts it
  • Reset links are one-use, expire in 15 minutes and sign everyone out
  • Phone app keeps only a rotating token in the phone's secure storage
  • New accounts wait for admin approval; nothing is granted until then

In place Rate limits

  • 120 requests a minute per address overall
  • Sign-in 20 a minute; reset emails 5 an hour per address and 3 per account
  • Registration 20 an hour per address, 3 per contact
  • Writes 60 a minute per user; uploads 10 a minute
  • Too many requests get a "slow down" answer, not an error

In place Who can see what

  • All 78 database tables have row-level security (checked live today)
  • The 66 private tables have no direct access at all; only checked database functions reach them
  • Every function checks the person's role and property assignment, never a value sent by the app
  • Owners see only their properties; cleaners see only their own cleans and photos
  • Bank details live in their own protected table, masked on screen

In place Data handling

  • Uploads: size caps, file type checked from the file's bytes, private storage
  • No keys or passwords in the code; platform passwords are refused by design
  • Requests from other websites are refused (exact origin check)
  • Pages cannot be framed; responses are not cached
  • Daily retention job; account deletion; data export
Live check today: Supabase's security advisor reports 0 errors. It reports 66 information notes (private tables with no direct access, which is intended) and 1 warning: leaked-password protection is off. See the first item below.

Deploy and quick checks

Everything built is on the main branch and the database changes are applied.

Deploy from Git Bash

git pull origin main
npm run build && npm run backend:deploy

If the lock file blocks the pull: git restore package-lock.json, then npm ci. Keep the deploy command as it is: it keeps your existing server settings.

After each deploy, check

  • Sign in as an owner, a cleaner and an admin
  • Open /privacy, /terms and /delete-account
  • Submit a clean with a required photo; try one without the photo
  • Switch the device offline, take a photo, go back online
  • Try a wrong password; confirm the message is the same for any account
