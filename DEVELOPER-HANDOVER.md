# Recharge developer handover

Start with `README.md`. It describes the parts of the platform and the commands.

## The old portal has been retired (7 October 2026)

The original portal ran on a separate Cloudflare D1 database and R2 file storage. It held no real
records and was never served by the deployed backend, so it has been removed:
- `/` now sends people to the live workspace (`/workspace`);
- the preview's `/legacy-preview` address redirects to the preview home;
- its screens, its APIs, the D1 schema and Drizzle migrations, and the D1 and R2 bindings are gone.

`docs/LEGACY-PORT-PLAN.md` records what each old centre did, and where that work now lives or is
planned.

## The app on phones (8 October 2026)

- **Home tools.** `lib/home-tools.ts` lists the tools each role sees first on Home. Each tool opens
  a Work view, a tab inside it (`sub`), or a section further down (`anchor`). Sections are found by
  their heading (`sectionAnchor("Daily checks")` is `section-daily-checks`), so renaming a
  `<Section title>` breaks a Home tool. `tests/home-tools.test.mjs` catches that.
- **Icons.** The illustrated icons are drawn in `mobile/src/DimensionalIcon.tsx`. `MODULE_ART`
  gives every tool its icon in the full tools list.
- **Speed.** The live build (`RECHARGE_LIVE_ONLY=1`, set by `scripts/build-workspace-web.mjs`)
  leaves out the sample-data preview (`mobile/App.tsx`). It uses resized logos
  (`mobile/assets/brand`) and Montserrat cut down to Latin characters (`mobile/assets/fonts`).
  The Worker tells browsers to keep content-hashed app files for a year
  (`hashedWorkspaceFile`). The welcome intro plays once a day per device, and it can be skipped.
- **Feedback.** Sheets wrap their "done" handler in `useSaved()` (`mobile/src/Celebrate.tsx`),
  so every successful save shows a short confirmation.

## Follow-ups without outside services (8 October 2026)

- **Home shortcuts** (`homeActionsFor` in `lib/home-tools.ts`) open a form straight away. The Work
  screen reads `focus.open` when it mounts (`useWorkFocus`).
- **Automatic refresh.** `LiveWorkspace` refreshes quietly every minute while open and when the
  app comes back to the front. It pauses while a form or sheet is open.
- **Update wording.** `lib/notice-copy.ts` titles every update in the app, and gives the subject and
  message for email and push. `integration_claim_notifications` now passes `eventType`.
- **Email/push only for recent notices.** `integration_claim_notifications` only creates email and push delivery rows for notices whose `workspace_activity` event is under 24 hours old, so switching the channels on never sends the backlog; older notices stay readable in the app.
- **Weekly report notice.** `workspace_weekly_report_notices()` runs on the backend schedule. It
  sends owners an in-app notice on Mondays from 8am UK time, once per property per week.
- **Repair quotes.** The `reviseQuote` step updates the quote. A higher quote above the owner's
  limit sends the repair back to `owner_approval` with a `resumePhase`. `approveBudget` returns it
  to that phase.
- **Garden routine.** An owner's "has a garden" answer also creates a monthly "Garden and bins"
  routine plan.
- **Agreement copy.** `lib/agreement-copy.ts` builds a page with the signed agreement.
- **Map.** The Portfolio map draws real geography: coastline, council areas, lochs, rivers and towns
  from Natural Earth (public domain), generated into `lib/scotland-geo.ts` by
  `scripts/build-scotland-map.mjs` (the header says how to re-run it). `lib/scotland-projection.ts`
  is the Mercator projection both the script and the app use. `lib/scotland-map.ts` places one pin
  per area (nudged so none overlap) and works out the zoom boxes; `mobile/src/ScotlandMap.tsx`
  draws it. No map tile service is called. Property dots come from `lib/property-points.ts`: a saved
  latitude/longitude if there is one; otherwise the position of the address's postcode from
  postcodes.io (free, no key; `postcodePositions` in `lib/postcode-lookup.ts` sends only the postcodes,
  100 a request, and the app caches them for the session), which shows the street but not the door;
  failing that, the postcode district, looked up in `lib/postcode-districts.ts` (generated by
  `scripts/build-postcode-districts.mjs` from open district outlines). Dots at one position fan out.
- **Native scrolling.** `Anchor` (`LiveKit`) registers sections so the phone app can scroll to them.

## Access control for super admins (8 October 2026)

- **Where:** Work → Management → Access control (`mobile/src/LiveAccessControl.tsx`). It's also on
  a super admin's Home.
- **Backend:** it uses the admin endpoints (sections `accessControl` and `accessHistory`; actions
  `setAccess`, `block`, `unblock`, `signOut`, `revokePassword` and `deleteAccount`). The database
  functions are `workspace_access_control_*`, and only super admins can call them. Nobody can act on
  their own account.
- **Revoke password:** adds a row to `private.workspace_password_revocations`, which
  `auth_server_finish_password` checks before approving a sign-in. Email or phone recovery clears it.
- **Block:** sets the status to `revoked`, ends sessions and sets `auth.users.banned_until`.
- **Delete:** removes the auth user when nothing refers to it. Otherwise the account is closed:
  "Deleted account", no access, banned, password revoked.
- **History:** every change is logged in `workspace_access_events`.
- **Changing a role:** the sheet lists every role with what it gives (`ROLE_SUMMARY`), and previews
  what the person will see (`roleChange`: Work views, Home tools, properties). The tools reset to the
  new role's usual set. `workspace_move_assignments` (migration `20261008290000`) moves property
  assignments to the new role's kind, because property access matches the assignment kind to the
  role. An owner becoming a team member, or the reverse, is unassigned. A `role_changed` event
  records the old and new role. The person's app reads the account returned with every refresh and
  switches to the new role without signing in again.
- **Applying it live:** migration `20261008280000_access_control.sql` removes sign-in sessions, so the
  database tool asks for confirmation. It is applied; the one-off paste scripts in `docs/` were
  removed because running them again would put back older versions of functions.

- **Messages** (`mobile/src/LiveChat.tsx`, `lib/workspace-chat-*.ts`, migration `20261009110000`):

- **Weekly report page** (`weeklyReportDocument` in `lib/weekly-report.ts`) and **local events**
  added by hand (`recordLocalEvent`).
- **Listings** per property with links and IDs (`saveListingLinks`, migration `20261009120000`).
- **Applying it live:** applied (the migrations in `supabase/migrations` are the record).

## Adding people and signing up (8 October 2026)

- **Add a person** (Access control, super admins): name, email and role. `addPerson` in
  `lib/account-invite-server.ts` creates the sign-in with the server key, already verified
  (`email_confirm`), then saves the role and the role's usual tools through
  `workspace_admin_action` as the super admin (so every database check applies; the sign-in is
  removed again if the database refuses). No email is sent and no password is set: the reply
  carries a one-time link, `/auth/set-password?token=…`, for the super admin to send privately.
- **Set-password link** (`passwordLink`): the same link for an existing account, from its sheet.
  Not for super admins, your own account, or blocked or deleted accounts. Both are recorded in
  the access history (`account_created`, `password_link_created`).
- **The link** opens `app/auth/set-password`, which uses the code only when the person presses the
  button (so chat-app link previews can't use it up). It POSTs to `/auth/recovery`, which accepts it
  only as a recovery (`verifyOtp` with `type: "recovery"`) and continues to `/update-password`, the
  same step as "Forgot password". The link lasts as long as Supabase Auth's email link lifetime
  (1 hour by default).
- **Create an account** on the sign-in screen appears only when `GET /api/mobile/account` reports
  `enabled` (Worker variable `MOBILE_REGISTRATION_ENABLED=true`). It needs custom SMTP in Supabase
  Auth (the built-in sender only reaches the project team) and the "Confirm signup" email template
  must show the code (`{{ .Token }}`). New accounts wait for approval in People or Access control.
  When it's off, the sign-in screen tells new people to ask their administrator.

## Daily work in bonuses, platform logins, guest inbox (9 October 2026)

Migration `20261009140000_daily_work_logins_inbox` (applied live).

  `workspace_blend_score`). Daily work is done ÷ (target × days on a guest or client relations
  shift), per item, capped at 100% and averaged. Targets per day and the weight (`work_weight`,

  one row per platform with login, holder, where the password is kept, who gets the security code.
  There is no password field, and text that looks like a typed password is refused
  (`lib/ota-accounts.ts`).
- **Guest inbox** (Stays → Guest inbox, `mobile/src/LiveGuestInbox.tsx`, `lib/guest-inbox-*`,
  `/api/{mobile,workspace}/guest-inbox`): `private.workspace_guest_threads` groups
  `workspace_guest_messages` (now with `body` and `delivery`). Staff log guest messages and save
  replies (`delivery = 'logged'`) for anything outside Hostaway; Hostaway's conversations arrive every
  minute through `workspace_hostaway_messages_ingest`, which calls `workspace_guest_messages_ingest`
  with `body`, `guestName`, `reservationRef`, `checkIn`, `checkOut` (see `docs/HOSTAWAY-SYNC.md`).

- **Favorites:** the star in search, on property and work sheets, and on the Favorites page saves

  `20261010260000_favorites`, applied live) through the teams route (`section=favorites`, action
  `saveFavorites` → `workspace_save_favorites`), so the same list appears on every device: up to
  40 keys such as `tool:queue` or `property:<id>`, each with a label. One way in on each device: the

  Help button (`FavoritesShortcut`, it shrinks away while scrolling); the bottom bar is unchanged.
  Search is in the header only (no second bar on Home). State lives in `mobile/src/Favorites.tsx`.
- **View as (super admins):** the eye button in the header, or Profile → "View the app as someone
  else" (`mobile/src/ViewAs.tsx`, `lib/view-as.ts`). The picker lists each role with its people.
  - **A person** shows exactly what they see. `startViewAs` (teams route → `workspace_view_as_start`)
    records the view in `private.workspace_view_as_sessions` and Access history ("Viewed as"), and
    returns their account. Every read then carries `x-recharge-view-as: <their id>`
    (`workspaceRequest` in `mobile/src/backend/client.ts`). On the server, `businessContext` in
    `lib/business-server.ts` builds a client with that header, for GET requests from active super
    admins only; POSTs always run as the super admin.
  - **In the database** (migration `20261010270000_view_as_person`, applied live),
    `public.workspace_request_check()` is the Data API's `pgrst.db_pre_request` and runs before
    every request. Without the header it returns at once. With it, it checks the caller is an active
    super admin with a view of that person started in the last 12 hours and not ended. It then
    swaps `auth.uid()` (the JWT `sub`) and the cached role for that one request, and makes the
    transaction read-only. Every RLS policy and function therefore answers as the person, with no
    change to them. While viewing, `workspace_chat_read` only shows conversations the super admin
    could open themselves, and `workspace_privacy_read` is refused. An ended view answers 409 and the
    app goes back to the super admin's own view.
  - **To switch it off:** `alter role authenticator reset pgrst.db_pre_request; notify pgrst,
    'reload config';`.
  - **A role nobody has yet** ("… screens only") shows that role's menus and tools with the super
    admin's own records (`previewAccount`).
  - **Either way**, `setViewOnly` refuses every write except this device's notification link. The
    choice lasts for the browser tab, and a banner offers "Back to my view".
- **Role change brings the role's tools:** migration `20261010250000_role_tools` (applied live).
  `private.workspace_role_tools` matches `DEFAULT_ROLE_MODULES` (a test checks this).
- **Messages** shows the department channels on every tab. **The Properties map** pans, zooms

- **Enquiry form helpers:** `lib/postcode-lookup.ts` (postcodes.io, free, only the postcode is sent)

- **Search on long lists:** `useListSearch` and `ListSearchBox` (`mobile/src/SearchPicker.tsx`) add a

- **View as, role preview:** Messages keeps only what the role could have

## Demo mode (10 October 2026)

- **What it is:** a super admin's Live / Demo switch (header on wide screens, and Profile). Demo shows a
  sample business on every screen; the real database is never read or written for it, and nothing
  saves (every send answers "Demo mode: nothing is saved…"). View as works inside the demo with its
  sample people. A banner and the left panel say it is on, with Switch to Live. It lasts for the tab.
- **How:** `workspaceRequest` (`mobile/src/backend/client.ts`) answers reads from the recording while
  `setDemo` is set (`demoAnswer` in `lib/demo-data.ts`): requests are matched with their dates made
  relative to the day of recording, and every date in the answers is moved to today, so the sample
  stays current. The recording is `mobile/web-static/demo-data.json`, served at
  `/workspace/demo-data.json` and fetched only when someone switches demo on.
- **Recording it again** (after schema or screen changes): run the local review backend with the sample
  business (`scripts/demo/`), then `node scripts/build-demo-data.mjs`. It replays the reads listed per role
  in `scripts/demo/requests.json` and writes the file. `tests/demo-data.test.mjs` checks it parses and

## Messages like a messenger: photos, video, reactions, groups, who is online, profiles (10 October 2026)

- **Photos and a video in a message**: up to 5 photos (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, 15 MB each) and 1 video
  (MP4, MOV, WebM, up to 50 MB), with or without words. The app asks for one-off upload links
  (`prepareFiles` on `/api/{workspace,mobile}/chat`, `lib/chat-media-server.ts`) and sends each file
  straight to the private `chat-files` bucket, so a 50 MB video never passes through the Worker.
  Sending claims the files; the database checks each stored object's size and type against the
  limits first (`private.chat_attach`). Files are opened with signed links valid for an hour
  (`/api/{workspace,mobile}/chat-files?ids=…`), only for people who can read the conversation.
  Files added but never sent are let go after 6 hours (and when taken out before sending).
  In the browser videos play inline; the phone app opens them in the phone's own player.
- **Reactions**: 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏 🎉 ✅, one per person per message (the same again takes it off).
  Hover a message (or long-press on phones) for the smiley; tap a reaction to add or take off yours.
- **Groups**: anyone on the team starts a group (a name is optional; unnamed groups are called
  after their people), sees who is in any conversation (the faces in the thread header), and adds
  people to a group they are in. Whoever started it, or an admin, removes people; anyone leaves.
  Adding people to a one-to-one starts a new group. Each change shows as a line in the group
  ("Fiona added Ewan"), never counted as unread or notified. Team channels list their whole
  department; their membership still follows the rota.
- **Who is online**: the app checks in once a minute while open and in view (`heartbeat`, never in
  the demo or while viewing as someone). A green dot shows on people seen in the last 2½ minutes;
  otherwise "Active 5m ago". Owners are not shown the team's comings and goings.
- **Your profile** (Profile & preferences → Your profile): a photo (JPG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB,
  checked from its bytes, kept in the private `avatars` bucket), a colour behind your initials and a
  line about you (160 characters). Names and emails still change only in Access control. Photos
  show in Messages, the toolbar and the left panel (`/api/{workspace,mobile}/profile?userId=…`).
  Deleting an account clears its photo and line; a copy of your data includes them.

## Report a message, block a person, Reported messages (12 October 2026)

For Apple App Review guideline 1.2 and Google Play's user-generated-content policy: a way to
report objectionable content, a way to block abusive users, and timely action on reports.

- **Report** (any conversation, from the `⋯` beside a message, or the `⋯` in the long-press
  reaction bar): Offensive or abusive, Harassment, Inappropriate photo or video, Spam, Something
  else, plus an optional note (500 characters). Not your own message; one open report per person
  per message; 10 an hour and 30 a day. The reporter sees "Thanks — an admin will review this."
  and is offered "Block". Every active admin and super admin is notified (`chat.reported`,
  copy in `lib/notice-copy.ts`). Reporting is a chat action (`reportMessage`, with a request id).
- **Block** (`blockUser` / `unblockUser`, read section `blocks`): from a message's menu, the
  people list of a conversation, or undone from Profile & preferences → Blocked people. Anyone
  can block anyone they share a conversation with (team members can block any team member; owners
  can block the team members who answer them, shown as "Recharge · Fiona").
  **Effect: for the blocker only.** The database withholds the blocked person's messages (no
  words, photos, videos or reactions are sent; the app shows "Message from someone you blocked"
  with an Unblock option), they are not unread and send no notification, and their photos cannot
  be opened by the blocker. Neither side can start a *new* direct conversation with the other
  (an existing one carries on; the blocker just never sees it). Blocking is silent. **Why this
  shape:** in groups, team channels, property and job chats and owner conversations a block must
  never stop someone doing their job or reaching the people they work with, so it is only ever
  a filter on the blocker's own view. Work is handed out through jobs and notices, not chat, so
  blocking an admin cannot stop work instructions; anyone who really must be kept out is dealt
  with by an admin (Access control → block the account). Event lines ("added Alex") are never hidden.
- **Reported messages** (admins and super admins; left panel → Management, Settings →
  Reported messages, search): Open, Removed, Dismissed, All, newest first. Each report shows the
  message (or "1 photo"), who wrote it, who reported it and why, how many people reported it and
  how often its sender has been reported. **Remove message** hides it for everyone (including
  its sender), replaced by "Removed by an admin", stops its reactions, and stops its photos and
  videos being opened; the route (`decideChatReport` in `lib/chat-media-server.ts`) also deletes
  the stored files, so signed links already handed out stop working too. **Dismiss report**
  leaves it. Either decides every open report on that message together. An admin cannot decide a
  report about their own message. While a report is open, admins may open its photos to judge it.
  Removed text is kept in the database (hidden) as evidence.
  Deciding also tells each reporter whose open report it closed (`chat.report_removed` or
  `chat.report_reviewed`) and, on removal, the sender (`chat.message_removed`); the notices carry no
  actor, so neither side learns which admin decided or who reported. Migration `20261012030000_chat_report_outcomes.sql`.
- Migration `20261012010000_chat_reports_blocks.sql` (`private.chat_reports`, `private.chat_blocks`,
  `chat_messages.removed_at/removed_by`; chat actions `reportMessage`, `blockUser`, `unblockUser`;
  chat read section `blocks`; team read section `chatReports`, team action `decideChatReport`).
  Existing functions are patched by anchor and the migration is safe to run twice. Screens:
  `mobile/src/ChatSafety.tsx`, `BlockedPeople.tsx`, `LiveModeration.tsx` (and `LiveChat.tsx`,
  `ChatParts.tsx`); shared rules `lib/chat-moderation.ts`; tests
  `tests/chat-moderation-database.test.mjs` and `tests/chat-moderation-policy.test.mjs`.
- Terms of use now say objectionable content is not allowed, reports are reviewed and abusive
  people are removed. The privacy page was not changed (it does not list reports; they hold the
  reporter's name, the reason and an optional note, and are kept as business records).
- Not done: telling the reporter or the sender what was decided; showing the messages around a
  reported one; a reports badge in the navigation (admins get the notice and the Open count);
  a seeded report in the demo business (Demo says "This part isn't in the demo business" until the
  demo data is recorded again; reporting and blocking are refused in Demo like every other save). **Admins should check Reported messages at least daily**; Apple expects action
  within 24 hours. Not yet applied to the live database.
